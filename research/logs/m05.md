### Where the three vendor guides agree and differ

**Agreement (all three, as read on 2026-09-28):** be specific about the task and the desired output format; give context rather than assume knowledge; use examples, with diversity mattering more than quantity; separate instruction from data using structure (headers/tags); iterate and test rather than accept the first attempt.

**Differences of emphasis:**
- **Anthropic** puts unusual weight on *explaining the reason* for an instruction (so the model generalises to unstated cases) and on XML-tag structuring.
- **Google** foregrounds *breaking a complex prompt into simpler ones* and experimenting with decoding parameters.
- **OpenAI** foregrounds *message roles and their differing authority*, prompt caching (put stable content first) and building evaluation suites before production.

These differences are reflected in the lectures: reason-giving in 5.1, structure in 5.3, decomposition deferred to Module 6, and evaluation as the spine of 5.4.
