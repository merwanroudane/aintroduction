## Research log

| Topic | Sources used | Key findings | Date verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| AGI definitions | Goertzel (2014), doi 10.2478/jagi-2014-0001 | Even inside the AGI research community there is no single definition; several schools coexist. | 2026-09-26 |
| Levels instead of a threshold | Morris et al. (arXiv:2311.02462, Google DeepMind) | Proposes a matrix of **performance × generality**, and explicitly separates capability from autonomy. | 2026-09-26 |
| Measuring intelligence | Chollet (arXiv:1911.01547) | Intelligence as **skill-acquisition efficiency** given limited priors and experience, rather than performance on trained skills. | 2026-09-26 |
| The "sparks" debate | Bubeck et al. (arXiv:2303.12712); Ullman (arXiv:2302.08399) | Bubeck et al. is explicitly exploratory; Ullman shows that trivial alterations to theory-of-mind tasks collapse performance, suggesting pattern reuse rather than robust reasoning. | 2026-09-26 |
| Expert forecasts | Grace et al. (arXiv:2401.02843) | Thousands of AI authors surveyed; **very wide dispersion** and sensitivity to question framing. | 2026-09-26 |
| Superintelligence | Bostrom (2014), OUP; Russell (2019), Human Compatible | Treated analytically in the risk and control literature; not a description of an existing or scheduled system. | 2026-09-26 |
| Four-type taxonomy | Hintze (2016), The Conversation | The popular source of the reactive / limited-memory / theory-of-mind / self-aware split; a teaching simplification, not a technical standard. | 2026-09-26 |
| Theory of mind origin | Premack & Woodruff (1978), doi 10.1017/S0140525X00076512 | The term comes from comparative psychology; transferring it to machines imports assumptions. | 2026-09-26 |
| Graded autonomy | SAE J3016 (2021) | Driving automation defined in six levels tied to functions: a model for treating autonomy as graded rather than binary. | 2026-09-26 |
| Emergence caveat | Schaeffer et al. (arXiv:2304.15004) | Apparent sudden emergence may be an artefact of discontinuous metrics. | 2026-09-26 |

## Modern extensions added

- **Levels of AGI** framing (Morris et al.) as a methodological improvement over the binary question.
- **Skill-acquisition efficiency** (Chollet) as an alternative measurement philosophy.
- **AI agents** anatomised (model, tools, state, loop, permissions) with five explicit differences from AGI, fulfilling the specification's requirement that an AI agent is not AGI.

## To verify / limitations

- Placements in `m03_scope_autonomy` and `m03_levels` are **teaching estimates** for discussion, explicitly labelled as such; they are not measured scores.
- The survey result is reported qualitatively (wide dispersion, framing sensitivity) rather than by quoting specific median years, which change between survey waves.
- "Most systems today are described as limited memory" is a statement about the popular taxonomy's own categories, not a measured census of deployed systems.
- Memory features of specific commercial assistants are deliberately **not** described here; they are product features that change and must be verified from official documentation in Module 7.
